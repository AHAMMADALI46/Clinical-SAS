Drug X 10 mg Bioequivalence Study
Overview
Documentation and analysis for a double‑blinded, randomized, two‑treatment, two‑period, two‑sequence, single‑dose, crossover oral bioequivalence study of Drug X 10 mg tablets in healthy adults under fasting conditions.

Study Details

Design: Double‑blinded, randomized, two‑treatment, two‑period, two‑sequence, single‑dose, crossover
Condition: Fasting
Sponsor: den PHARMA Private Limited, India
CRO: APS Tech India Pvt. Limited
Submission Country: USA
Objectives

Evaluate bioequivalence of Drug X 10 mg (den PHARMA) vs CARE® (Drug X) 10 mg (Ast Pharma US).
Monitor adverse events and ensure subject safety.
Participants

Total: 48 healthy adults (18–45 years, BMI 18.5–29.9 kg/m²)
Washout Period: 21 days
Drug Products

Test (T): Drug X 10 mg – Well Biopharma Pvt. Ltd., India
Reference (R): CARE® (Drug X) 10 mg – Ast Pharma Technologies, USA
Procedures

Single oral dose after ≥ 10 hours fasting (240 mL water).
23 blood samples per period (0–72 h).
Vital signs, ECG, and adverse events monitored.
Pharmacokinetics & Statistics

Primary PK: Cmax, AUC₀–₇₂
Secondary PK: Tmax
Bioequivalence Range: 90% CI within 80–125% for Cmax and AUC₀–₇₂
Ethics & Compliance

Conducted per ICH‑GCP, USFDA, and Declaration of Helsinki.
IEC approval and written informed consent obtained.
